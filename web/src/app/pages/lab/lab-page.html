Card becomes the page

Press a card: it shrinks a little, then its picture grows into the hero and the title moves into the page title. Back shrinks it into the same card and puts focus on it.

Menu grows from its button

Open it: the button turns into the panel, the page dims to 40% and the items come in one by one. Closing shrinks it back into the button.
Esc or the dim area closes it and focus goes back to the button. “Bouncy” overshoots on open, bounces each item in and pulls back before it closes.

Small motions
